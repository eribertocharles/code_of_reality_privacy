Code of Reality

Privacy Policy

Effective August 11, 2026

Code of Reality does not collect your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no third-party SDKs, and no servers. The app contains no networking code at all. Everything it makes stays on your iPhone until you choose to share it.

What the app uses while running

Camera and LiDAR scanner. Code of Reality reads the camera image and the LiDAR depth sensor to find the shape of the room around you and draw on top of it. This happens live, on the device.

Device motion. The app reads the accelerometer and gyroscope to keep tracking steady in the dark (Night Mode). This is the orientation and movement of the phone only. It is not activity or fitness tracking, and it is not location.

Photo library. The app never reads your photo library. It can add to it, and only in one situation: if you export a scan and then choose “Save Image” or “Save Video” in the iOS share sheet, iOS asks for permission to add that one file. Nothing else is written, and nothing existing is read.

The app does not use location services, the microphone, your contacts, or health data.

What the app saves on your iPhone

When you save a scan, Code of Reality writes a scene file into its own private storage. A scene contains:

  • the 3D shape of the scanned room, and the colors sampled from the camera
  • the path you walked while scanning
  • Apple's ARKit world map, which lets the app recognize the room again later
  • a small number of photographs — low-resolution stills (640 pixels or less) captured during the scan, which the app shows as a faded guide so you can line the phone back up when continuing a scan
  • a thumbnail image of the finished scene, for the gallery

Those photographs are pictures of your room. They never leave the device on their own, but they are worth knowing about.

Scene files live in the app's own storage, which other apps cannot read. Deleting the app deletes every scene with it. You can also delete individual scans in the gallery.

Backups. Because scenes are stored in the app's Documents area, they are included in an iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone, the same way most app data is. Those backups are governed by Apple's privacy policy and your own backup settings, not by this one.

What leaves your iPhone

Nothing, unless you choose to send it.

The app can export a scan as a picture (PNG) or as a short video (MP4) and hands it to the standard iOS share sheet. You pick the destination — Messages, Mail, AirDrop, Files, or another app. What happens to the file after that is governed by whichever service you chose. If a scan shows the inside of your home, please consider who you are sending it to.

Third parties

There are none. Code of Reality contains no analytics, no crash-reporting service, no advertising identifier, and no tracking of any kind. Nothing about your use of the app is transmitted to the developer or to anyone else.

Children

Code of Reality does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date. Because the app collects nothing, changes are expected to be rare.
