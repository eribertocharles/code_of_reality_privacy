# Privacy Policy — Code of Reality

**Effective August 9, 2026**

## The short version

Code of Reality does not collect your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no
advertising, no third-party SDKs, and no servers. The app has no networking code in it
at all. Everything it makes stays on your iPhone until you decide to share it.

## What the app uses while it is running

**Camera and LiDAR scanner.** Code of Reality reads the camera image and the LiDAR
depth sensor to find the shape of the room around you and draw on top of it. This
happens live, on the device.

**Device motion.** The app reads the accelerometer and gyroscope to keep tracking
steady in the dark (Night Mode). This is orientation and movement of the phone only.
It is not activity or fitness tracking, and it is not location.

The app does not use GPS or location services, the microphone, contacts, your photo
library, or your health data.

## What the app saves on your iPhone

When you save a scan, Code of Reality writes a scene file into its own private storage
area. A scene contains:

- the 3D shape of the scanned room, and the colors sampled from the camera
- the path you walked while scanning
- Apple's ARKit "world map," which lets the app recognize the room again later
- a small number of **photographs** — low-resolution still images (640 pixels or less)
  taken from the camera during the scan, which the app shows you as a faded guide so
  you can line the phone back up when you continue a scan
- a thumbnail picture of the finished scene, for the gallery

Those photographs are pictures of your room. They never leave the device on their own,
but they are worth knowing about.

Scene files live in the app's own storage, which other apps cannot read. Deleting the
app deletes every scene with it. You can also delete individual scenes in the gallery.

**Backups.** Because scenes are stored in the app's Documents area, they are included
in an iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone, the same way most app data is. Those
backups are covered by Apple's privacy policy and your own backup settings, not by
this one.

## What leaves your iPhone

Nothing, unless you choose to send it.

The app can export a scene as a 3D model (USDZ) or as a picture (PNG), and hands it to
the standard iOS share sheet. At that point you pick the destination — Messages, Mail,
AirDrop, Files, or another app. What happens to the file after that is governed by
whichever service you chose. If a scan shows the inside of your home, please think
about who you are sending it to.

## Third parties

There are none. Code of Reality contains no analytics, no crash-reporting service, no
advertising identifier, and no tracking of any kind. Nothing about your use of the app
is transmitted to the developer or to anyone else.

## Children

Code of Reality does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective
date. Because the app collects nothing, changes are expected to be rare.
